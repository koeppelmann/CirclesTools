Reads on-chain state from Gnosis Chain every few seconds. The countdown ticks locally between refreshes. A bid is 100 CRC: 10 grows the pool, 10 goes to the owner, 80 pays the FIFO claim queue (each bid mints a 110 CRC claim). When the clock hits zero, anyone can settle and the last bidder is paid the pool.
